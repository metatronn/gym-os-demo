# Task 040: Executive Dashboards, Franchise Templates & White-Label Controls

**Phase:** 10 — Workspace, Memory & Brand Intelligence
**Priority:** High
**Depends on:** [025](./025-reports-real.md), [030](./030-multi-location-architecture.md), [039](./039-knowledge-brand-memory.md)
**Blocks:** [044](./044-predictive-analytics.md)

---

## Objective

Add the executive and franchise-management layer described in the docs: consolidated cross-location reporting, reusable global templates, brand governance, and white-label controls for franchise-style operators.

## Steps

### 1. Executive Views

Build dashboards for:

- membership growth by location
- revenue by location
- retention and churn by location
- campaign performance by location
- staffing and class utilization comparisons

### 2. Global Templates

Support template systems for:

- billing defaults
- schedule templates
- waivers/forms
- campaign templates
- onboarding templates for new locations

### 3. White-Label / Brand Controls

Enable org or franchise operators to manage:

- logos and visual brand assets
- brand copy defaults
- location-specific overrides
- feature visibility by brand/operator type

### 4. Rollout Controls

Support change management across locations:

- publish template changes centrally
- preview impact before rollout
- apply to one, many, or all locations
- preserve local overrides where allowed

## Acceptance Criteria

- Multi-location operators can compare locations from executive dashboards
- Reusable operational templates exist for common rollout scenarios
- Brand defaults and location overrides are supported
- White-label/franchise controls are represented in the product model
- The system can onboard additional locations faster than manual one-off setup
